Ask listeners about the most ridiculous rule their employer had to create because…New

Ask listeners about the most ridiculous rule their employer had to create because somebody actually did something stupid. Examples might include: A company banning microwaving fish. A sign explaining how to use the restroom. A rule prohibiting employees from sleeping in their cars. A ban on bringing certain pets to work. Best question: "What's a rule at your workplace that exists because ONE idiot ruined it for everybody?" How Long Have You Known Your Halloween Costume? "Halloween Overachievers." Ask listeners about the most outrageous amount of time or money they've spent creating a costume. The question: "What's the most ridiculous thing you've ever done to win a Halloween costume contest?" . The Thing You Refuse to Replace Everyday, relatable phone topic "What's something in your house that's been broken for years, but you've learned to live with it?" The car window you can't roll down. The microwave that only works if you slam the door. The light switch nobody understands.

"What's a secret test you've given your spouse, boyfriend, girlfriend or friend to see…New

"What's a secret test you've given your spouse, boyfriend, girlfriend or friend to see whether they really care?" Examples: Did you stop texting first? Did you deliberately leave a chore undone? Did you mention something you wanted for your birthday and wait to see whether they remembered? Did you stop making plans to see whether someone else would make the effort? The best follow-up: "Did they pass—and do they know you were testing them?" This could become a two-break topic because listeners may start recognizing tests their partners have been giving them.
