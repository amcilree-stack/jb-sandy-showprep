The 30-Second Job Nobody Will DoNew

The 30-Second Job Nobody Will Do Type: Everyday, relatable phone topic. The setup "Have you ever noticed that every household has one tiny chore that absolutely nobody wants to do?" It takes practically no effort. But somehow everybody walks past it for days. The empty toilet-paper roll. The overflowing trash can. The dishwasher that needs unloading. The lightbulb that burned out three months ago. The Amazon box that's been sitting by the front door for two weeks. The opening question "What's the smallest job in your house that everybody refuses to do?"

"I'm Never Learning That!"New

"I'm Never Learning That!" Opening question: "What's something everybody thinks you should know how to do, but you've decided you're never going to learn?" Maybe you refuse to learn how to change a tire. You have no interest in learning how to cook. You don't know how to use your television remote. You refuse to learn how to back up a trailer. Or you still can't figure out how to use your phone's calendar. The twist: Ask callers how old they are. A 65-year-old who has never learned how to operate a dishwasher might have a great story.
