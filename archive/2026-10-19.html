Show ideas

"What's a secret test you've given your spouse, boyfriend, girlfriend or friend to see…

Mon, Oct 19

"What's a secret test you've given your spouse, boyfriend, girlfriend or friend to see whether they really care?" Examples: Did you stop texting first? Did you deliberately leave a chore undone? Did you mention something you wanted for your birthday and wait to see whether they remembered? Did you stop making plans to see whether someone else would make the effort? The best follow-up: "Did they pass—and do they know you were testing them?" This could become a two-break topic because listeners may start recognizing tests their partners have been giving them.

Mon, Oct 19

"If you and your spouse were flying together and only one of you got upgraded to first class, who gets the seat?" Make it more interesting by presenting three scenarios: A 90-minute flight. A four-hour flight. A ten-hour international flight.

“Without telling us what your spouse does for a living, tell us what they smell like when…

Mon, Oct 19

“Without telling us what your spouse does for a living, tell us what they smell like when they get home.” Gasoline, french fries, dogs, hospital sanitizer, sawdust, chlorine, beer, barbecue smoke. Then the show guesses the profession.
