“Things We Did as Kids That Parents Would Never Allow Today.”

Mon, Nov 2

“Things We Did as Kids That Parents Would Never Allow Today.” Opening question: “What's something your parents let you do at age 10 that would get somebody reported today?” Riding bicycles across town. Staying out until dark. Going to the movies without an adult. The twist: Ask listeners what age they were when they first stayed home alone. Why it works: Especially well suited to a multigenerational Austin audience.

Added Nov 2

Older than 7 days (added Nov 2)

“Austin's Department of Fun.”

Mon, Nov 2

“Austin's Department of Fun.” Appoint one host as the self-proclaimed Commissioner of Fun. Each Monday, the commissioner must propose one inexpensive activity listeners can try that week. The other host determines whether the idea sounds fun or miserable. “The Austin Complaint Song.” Choose one ridiculous local frustration each week and turn it into a short original comedy song. Possible subjects: construction cones, parking tickets, grocery-store checkout lines or confusing neighborhood street names. I've Been Doing It Wrong My Entire Life Type: Everyday listener-call topic. Opening question: “What's something you recently discovered you've been doing wrong for years?” Maybe you've been using a kitchen tool incorrectly. Mispronouncing a familiar word. Taking an unnecessarily complicated route to work. Or misunderstanding a feature on your car.
