Show ideas

The closest you’ve come to getting arrested

Sat, Sep 12

“Which Cast Members Got Arrested!?” and a Worst Date Ever involving an arrest. Don’t copy the benchmark—use the broader question: “What is the closest you have ever come to getting arrested?” College prank, public intoxication scare, mistaken identity, traffic incident, dumb youthful decision. Keep it to nonviolent/minor stories and the calls should be fantastic.

The hobby that suddenly got cool again

Sat, Sep 12

Mahjong Is Suddenly Everywhere—Use the Hobby Nobody Expected to Become Cool., while current mahjong leagues/classes around the country are selling out or expanding. The radio topic is: “What hobby suddenly became cool again?” Mahjong, pickleball, knitting, vinyl, film cameras, gardening, birding. Austin twist: make JB and Sandy each learn one “unexpectedly hot” hobby for a week and report back.

What dead Austin business would you bring back?

Tue, Sep 8

“What store would you bring back tomorrow if you could?” Blockbuster, Tower Records, Toys “R” Us in its old form, Circuit City, Foley’s, old mall stores. Then localize it: “What dead Austin business would you resurrect?” That second hour could be excellent for heritage listeners.What Dead Austin Business Would You Bring Back? This feels strongest for immediate phones because it uses JB & Sandy’s history in the city and gives listeners a nostalgia lane syndicated shows can’t duplicate.

The struggle meal you didn’t know was a struggle meal

Tue, Sep 8

“What meal did your family eat because it was cheap that you didn’t realize was a ‘struggle meal’ until you were older?” Buttered noodles, beans and rice, breakfast for dinner, canned chili, hot dogs cut into macaroni. Then ask: “Do you STILL love it?” That second question brings nostalgia rather than embarrassment.
