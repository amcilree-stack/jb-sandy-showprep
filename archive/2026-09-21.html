Your Optimus gets ONE chore forever

Thu, Sep 24

What Job Would You Immediately Give to a Robot? Greg & The Morning Buzz discussed the increasing presence of robots Wednesday and asked which household jobs people would happily surrender—cleaning, litter boxes, snow removal, cooking. Amazon Music Austin twist: “Tesla gives you an Optimus tomorrow—but it can perform only ONE chore forever. What are you programming it to do?” Laundry? Yard? Dishes? H-E-B run? I think the interesting argument is whether you choose the most time-consuming chore or the one you simply hate most.

The job that looks awesome but sucks

Thu, Sep 24

The Job Everyone Thinks Is Awesome—But Actually Sucks. Preston & Steve asked listeners Wednesday about overly romanticized jobs. Podscan Great topic if you require firsthand experience: “What job looks amazing from the outside but you’ve actually done it—and it’s terrible?” Bartender, flight attendant, radio personality, chef, realtor, teacher, musician, working on a boat. The rule: you must have actually held the job. That eliminates uninformed complaining.

What the next generation may not know how to do

Thu, Sep 24

hat Are You Genuinely Worried the Next Generation Won’t Be Able to Do? Valentine also asked listeners about concerns for the next generation, including AI and shrinking attention spans. Sunny 105.3 Avoid the predictable “kids today” discussion. Ask: “What skill did everybody your age learn that you’re genuinely not sure your kids will ever need—or know how—to do?” Read a map, write cursive, drive a stick, use a paper dictionary, change a tire, memorize phone numbers, make change. Then younger listeners get to nominate skills JB & Sandy’s generation cannot do.

“What’s in Your Bag?”—But Turn It Into a Personality Test. Mojo in the Morning had people reveal what they carry around every day and the stories behind those items. Mojo in the Morning JB & Sandy could make this “Empty Your Pockets.” Without warning, every person in the studio empties pockets/purse/backpack onto the table.
