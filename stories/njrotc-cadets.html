Lone Star NJROTC Cadets Set Sights on Dual September Physical Challenges

From the Today/News Doc · clock started Sun Sep 6, 12:03 PM

The Lone Star Company NJROTC cadets are charging hard into a heavy physical training season as they lock in coordinates for back-to-back community events this month. The group will first stand out as frontline volunteers for the upcoming local 9/11 Heroes Run on September 13, before deploying their top fitness squads to compete in the intensive Tiger Challenge field meet at Stony Point High School on September 19.
