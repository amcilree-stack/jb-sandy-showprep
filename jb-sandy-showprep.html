Are You Genuinely Worried the Next Generation Won’t Be Able to Do? Valentine also asked listeners about concerns for the next generation, including AI and shrinking attention spans. Sunny 105.3 Avoid the predictable “kids today” discussion. What Are You SOOO EXTRA About?New

What Are You SOOO EXTRA About? The show asked listeners about areas of life where they put in considerably more effort than necessary. Johnjay and Rich Why it works: It's a personality-revealing topic that encourages people to defend their strange priorities. JB & Sandy adaptation: "I Know It's Ridiculous, But..." Ask listeners what ordinary activity they've turned into a major production. Examples: elaborate coffee preparation, perfectly arranged luggage, extreme car-washing routines, complicated lawn care or bringing professional equipment to a child's sporting event. The question: "What's something you spend way too much time or money on that everyone else thinks is ridiculous?" This would be particularly good for JB and Sandy to answer personally. Why it works: People are surprisingly willing to admit they participate in things solely…New

Why it works: People are surprisingly willing to admit they participate in things solely because everyone else seems to love them. JB & Sandy adaptation: "I Don't Actually Like That." Give listeners permission to admit they dislike something everyone around them loves. Austin examples might include waiting in line for barbecue, crowded music festivals, floating the river, camping or tailgating in extreme heat. The follow-up: "How long have you been pretending to enjoy it?"

Added Oct 13

How Expired Is Too Expired?New

How Expired Is Too Expired? Why it works: Everyone has a different tolerance for questionable food. JB & Sandy adaptation: "Would You Still Eat It?" Present food items with increasingly alarming dates. A sealed bag of chips expired six months ago. Yogurt expired yesterday. Leftover pizza from Thursday. A can of soup from 2019. JB and Sandy must choose whether they'd eat it. Phone question: "What's the oldest food you've eaten and lived to tell about?" Keep the segment hypothetical rather than encouraging listeners to consume unsafe food.

Supposedly The Greatest Pick Up Line Ever First Ever Human vs Robot Fight: Full story HERE What happened The robots were remote-controlled. Two models were named: a six-foot-tall EngineAI T800 and a Unitree H1. The author said the T800’s kicks were hard to dodge and that the H1’s balance made it difficult to knock down. The human fighter took real hits to the body. The author described feeling fear for the fighter and relief when it ended. Author’s points Robot-versus-robot fights feel different because the machines are not conscious and do not feel pain. The author said this could be the start of a new entertainment genre. For a fight against a robot with real AI perception, the author said the robots would need to be less powerful, throttled down, and compliant, or the result would be serious injury or worse. The author said this should be thought through before regulators use moments like this to restrict humanoid robotics.
